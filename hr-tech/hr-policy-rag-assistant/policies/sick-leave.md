# Sickness absence

## Reporting sick
If you are unable to work because of illness, inform your line manager before your shift or working day starts, by phone or through the HR portal. Shift workers also inform the shift lead. You do not need to say what the illness is.

## Medical certificate
A medical certificate of incapacity for work is required from the fourth calendar day of illness. Doctors in Germany transmit the certificate electronically to the health insurer, and HR retrieves it; you only need to tell your manager that you saw a doctor. Your manager may ask for a certificate from the first day in individual cases.

## Continued pay
Employees receive their full pay for up to six weeks of incapacity caused by the same illness. After six weeks, the statutory health insurer pays sickness benefit. The company tops up the sickness benefit to 90 percent of net pay until the end of the twelfth week for employees with more than two years of service.

## Illness during annual leave
If you fall ill during annual leave and have a medical certificate for those days, the days of illness are not counted as leave and are credited back to your leave balance. Send the certificate details to HR within one week of returning.

## Returning to work
After more than six weeks of absence within twelve months, HR offers a return-to-work conversation (occupational integration management). Taking part is voluntary.
