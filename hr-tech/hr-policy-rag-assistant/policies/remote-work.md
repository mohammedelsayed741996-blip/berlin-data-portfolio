# Mobile work

## Who can work remotely
Employees in office roles may work remotely for up to two days per week, with their line manager's agreement. Roles in production, logistics, maintenance and quality on the line are not eligible, because the work has to be done on site.

## Working from another country
Remote work from another EU country is possible for up to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from HR at least four weeks in advance, because of social security and tax rules. Remote work from outside the EU is not permitted.

## Equipment and security
The company provides a laptop and a headset. Employees work only on company devices, connect through the VPN, and keep confidential documents out of public view. A one-off allowance of 150 euros is paid for setting up a home workspace.

## Availability
During core hours, remote workers are reachable by chat and phone in the same way as in the office. Team meetings that are marked on-site take priority over remote days.
