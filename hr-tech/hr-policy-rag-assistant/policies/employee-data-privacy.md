# How we handle employee data

## What we process
HR processes the employee data needed for the employment relationship: contact details, contract and pay data, working time records, absences, and data required by law, such as tax and social security information. Health information is limited to what the law requires, for example the dates of incapacity on a medical certificate, not the diagnosis.

## Who can see it
Access is limited to people who need it for their role. Line managers see working time, absences and leave balances of their team, not pay data. HR systems log every access to personal files.

## How long we keep it
Applications from candidates who were not hired are deleted six months after the end of the recruiting process. Personnel files are kept for the duration of employment and deleted after the statutory retention periods, usually ten years for payroll records.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, have incorrect data corrected and, where the law allows, have data deleted. Requests go to the data protection officer at privacy@northlake-motors.example, who answers within one month.
