# Performance reviews and calibration (manager guide)

## Annual cycle
Managers hold a performance review with every team member in January and February, covering goals, results and development. Reviews are documented in the HR portal by 28 February.

## Rating scale
Performance is rated on a five-point scale from 1 (below expectations) to 5 (exceptional). As a guide, about 15 percent of a department receive a 4 and about 5 percent a 5; there is no forced distribution.

## Calibration
Ratings are calibrated in a meeting per department with the HR business partner in the first two weeks of March, before they are shared with employees. Managers bring evidence for every rating of 1, 4 or 5.

## Performance improvement plans
A performance improvement plan runs for at least 90 days, with written goals and a check-in every two weeks. HR must be involved before a plan is started.
