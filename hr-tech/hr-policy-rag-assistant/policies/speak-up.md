# Speak-up and whistleblowing

## What to report
Report suspected breaches of law or of our code of conduct, for example fraud, corruption, safety violations, discrimination or harassment. You do not need proof; a reasonable suspicion is enough.

## How to report
Reports can be made to your manager, to HR, or through the internal reporting channel, which is available online at any time, also anonymously, and by phone during office hours. The channel is run by Compliance and is separate from HR systems.

## What happens next
Compliance confirms receipt of a report within seven days and informs the person who reported about the measures taken within three months. Anonymous reporters receive updates through the reporting channel's secure mailbox.

## Protection
Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited and is itself a disciplinary matter. The identity of the person who reported is shared only with the people handling the case.
