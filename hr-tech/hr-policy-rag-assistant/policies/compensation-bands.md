# Salary bands and merit increases (HR internal)

## Salary bands
Salary bands are defined per job level from L1 to L8. Every band has a minimum, a midpoint and a maximum, and the maximum is 1.4 times the minimum. New hires are placed between the minimum and the midpoint; offers above the midpoint need approval from the HR director.

## Merit budget
The merit increase budget for 2026 is 3.5 percent of the base salary sum of each department. Employees rated 4 or 5 receive priority, and increases above 8 percent for a single employee need approval from the HR director.

## Pay equity review
Before increases are released, Compensation and Benefits checks every department for unexplained pay differences between groups doing equal work, in line with the Pay Transparency Act, and corrects them from a separate budget of 0.5 percent.
