# Business travel and expenses

## Booking
All business trips are booked through the travel tool after the line manager has approved the trip. Rail is the default for trips within Germany, in second class, and first class only for journeys over four hours. Flights are allowed only if the train journey would take more than six hours.

## Hotels
Hotels are booked through the travel tool up to 120 euros per night, or 160 euros per night in Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt and capital cities abroad. Higher rates need approval from the cost centre owner.

## Meal allowances
For trips within Germany the company pays the statutory per diem: 14 euros for an absence of more than 8 hours and 28 euros for a full 24-hour day. If a meal is provided, for example by the hotel or the customer, the per diem is reduced accordingly.

## Claiming expenses
Expenses are claimed in the expense tool with photos of the receipts within three months of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than three months cannot be reimbursed. Reimbursement is paid with the next salary run.
