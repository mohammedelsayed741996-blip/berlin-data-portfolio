# Parental leave and family support

## Parental leave
Each parent may take up to three years of parental leave per child. Leave that starts before the child's third birthday must be requested in writing at least seven weeks before it begins. Requests are submitted in the HR portal, which creates the written notice for you.

## Part-time during parental leave
During parental leave, employees may work part-time for up to 32 hours per week. The request is discussed with the line manager and HR at least seven weeks before the part-time work starts.

## Company top-up for partners
In addition to the statutory entitlements, the second parent receives two weeks of paid partner leave at full pay, to be taken within the first three months after the birth.

## Returning to work
After parental leave, employees return to their previous role or an equivalent one with the same pay. HR schedules a planning conversation about three months before the return date.

## Childcare
The company reserves places at a partner daycare centre near the site. Applications are made through the family service on the HR portal.
