# Commuting and mobility benefits

## Shuttle buses
Free shuttle buses run between Erkner station and the site, timed to the start and end of each shift. The timetable is published in the employee app.

## Public transport ticket
The company subsidises the Deutschlandticket with 30 euros per month. The ticket is ordered through the employee app and the remaining amount is deducted from the salary.

## Bike leasing
Employees can lease up to two bikes or e-bikes through salary conversion. The lease runs for 36 months, and the company pays the insurance. Applications are made in the benefits portal.

## Parking
Parking at the site is free. Charging points for electric cars are available in car parks P2 and P4 at a reduced employee rate.
