# Annual leave and special leave

## Entitlement
Employees on a five-day week receive 28 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees on a six-day shift pattern receive 33 working days. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to the number of days they work per week. In the year an employee joins or leaves, the entitlement is one twelfth of the annual amount for each full month of employment.

## Requesting leave
Leave is requested in the HR portal. Requests for more than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance, shorter requests at least three working days in advance. The line manager approves or declines within five working days. Production teams publish a leave plan for the summer shutdown weeks by 31 January.

## Carry-over
Leave that cannot be taken in the calendar year may be carried over until 31 March of the following year. Leave that is still open after 31 March expires, unless it could not be taken because of illness or parental leave, in which case it is kept for up to 15 months after the end of the leave year.

## Special leave
Employees receive paid special leave for the following events: their own wedding or civil partnership, 1 day; the birth of their child, 2 days; the death of a spouse, partner, child or parent, 2 days; moving house for work reasons, 1 day. Special leave must be taken within two weeks of the event and is requested in the HR portal with a supporting document.
