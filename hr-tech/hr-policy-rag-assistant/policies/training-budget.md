# Learning and development

## Individual budget
Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per calendar year for courses, certificates and conferences related to their current or next role. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Approval
Training is requested in the learning platform and approved by the line manager. Training that takes place during working time counts as working time.

## German language courses
German courses from level A1 to C1 are offered on site free of charge and do not count towards the individual budget. Classes take place before and after the early shift so that shift workers can attend.

## Repayment clause
For training that costs more than 3,000 euros, a repayment agreement is signed before the course. If the employee resigns within 24 months of finishing the training, the costs are repaid pro rata, reduced by one twenty-fourth for each full month after the course.
