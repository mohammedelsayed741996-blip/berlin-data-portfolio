# Working time, shifts and overtime

## Regular working time
The regular working time is 40 hours per week. In production, the three-shift model runs early shift 06:00 to 14:00, late shift 14:00 to 22:00 and night shift 22:00 to 06:00, each with a 30-minute unpaid break. Working time is recorded at the terminals at the hall entrances.

## Flexitime for office roles
Office employees work flexitime between 07:00 and 19:00, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 on Monday to Thursday and from 10:00 to 13:00 on Friday. Time outside the core hours can be arranged freely with the team.

## Overtime
Overtime must be approved by the line manager before it is worked, except in emergencies at the line, where the shift lead approves it and the manager confirms it afterwards. The working day must not exceed 10 hours including overtime, and at least 11 hours of rest are required between two shifts.

## Working time account
Hours above the regular working time go to the working time account. The account may hold up to 80 plus hours and must not fall below 20 minus hours. Hours above 80 are paid out with the next salary at the regular hourly rate plus the overtime premium of 25 percent, unless the employee asks to take them as time off within three months.
