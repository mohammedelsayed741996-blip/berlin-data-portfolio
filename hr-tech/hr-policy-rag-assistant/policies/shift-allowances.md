# Shift and weekend allowances

## Allowance rates
Hours worked on the late shift between 20:00 and 22:00 carry an allowance of 15 percent of the hourly base rate. Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 carries 25 percent. Work on Sundays carries 50 percent and work on public holidays 100 percent. On 24 and 31 December from 14:00 the public holiday rate applies.

## Combining allowances
If several allowances apply to the same hour, only the highest one is paid. The overtime premium is paid in addition to a shift allowance.

## Payment and tax
Allowances are calculated from the recorded working time and paid with the salary of the following month. Night, Sunday and public holiday allowances are tax-free within the statutory limits; the payslip shows them separately.
