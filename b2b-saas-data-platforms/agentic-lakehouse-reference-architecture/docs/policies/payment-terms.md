## Standard terms
Customers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland pay within 30, 45 or 60 days of the invoice date, as agreed in the framework contract. Customers in the Gulf region are invoiced in UAE dirhams and pay within the same term options.

## Early payment discount
Customers who pay within 10 days of the invoice date may deduct 2 percent. The discount is not available on invoices that are already overdue.

## Currency
Invoices to Gulf customers are issued in AED. Reports convert them to euros at the Finance month-end rate, so euro figures can move slightly when rates change.
