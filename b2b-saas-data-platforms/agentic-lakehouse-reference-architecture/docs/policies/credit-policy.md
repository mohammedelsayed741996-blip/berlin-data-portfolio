## Credit limits
Every account has a credit limit set by Finance: 250,000 euros for key accounts, 80,000 euros for mid-size accounts and 25,000 euros for small accounts, adjusted by up to 20 percent after the annual credit review. The limit applies to the total of open invoices, overdue or not.

## When an account goes over its limit
If open invoices exceed the credit limit, new orders above 5,000 euros need approval from the Finance team before they are confirmed. The account manager is informed automatically and agrees a payment plan with the customer.

## Credit hold
Finance may put an account on credit hold when invoices are more than 60 days past due or the account is above 120 percent of its limit. On hold, only prepaid orders are delivered. A credit hold is always proposed by Finance and confirmed by the Head of Finance; account managers cannot place or lift a hold.
