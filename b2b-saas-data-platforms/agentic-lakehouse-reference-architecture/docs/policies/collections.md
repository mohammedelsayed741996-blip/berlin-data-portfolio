## Reminder steps
The first reminder is sent 7 days after the due date, the second after 21 days, and the final notice after 45 days. Reminders are sent by the account manager to the customer's accounts payable contact, never to individual employees of the customer.

## Tone and content
Reminders list the open invoices with numbers, due dates and amounts, state the total overdue, and ask for payment or a reply within 10 days. They do not threaten legal steps; that decision belongs to Finance after the final notice.

## Approval
Every reminder drafted with the assistant is reviewed and approved by the account manager before it is sent. Finance approves final notices.
